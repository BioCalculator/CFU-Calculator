What is CFU/ml?

CFU/ml (Colony Forming Units per millilitre) is a measure of the number of bacterial cells in a liquid culture that are capable of forming colonies.

How is CFU/ml calculated here?

This calculator estimates CFU/ml from spectrophotometer readings using optical density (O.D.).

CFU/ml = (CFU per 1 O.D.) × (Measured O.D.)

Why is the default 8 × 108?

For E. coli, an optical density of 1.0 at OD600 typically corresponds to approximately 8 × 108 CFU/ml under standard laboratory conditions.

This value is widely used as a biological approximation and may vary depending on strain, growth conditions, and instrument calibration.
